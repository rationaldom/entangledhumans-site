I have led life as a professional student with a focus on Physics, Biology, and Computation. I studied the visual arts and photography as well, just not professionally. As a child I poured over Art books and copied master drawings. I roamed the The Art Institute of Chicago like a feral child. In graduate school was fortunate to spend a year in Berlin. I captured that year in Ektachrome and Tri-X on my trusty Pentax ME Super. Then the arts took a back seat as I settled into the joys and stresses of professional and family life. But now I am retired. My children are grown and my passion for the arts has returned with a vengeance.  I explore portrait, fashion, artistic-nude, and fetish photography.
